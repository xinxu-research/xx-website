Handbook of Meta-Research

Edited by Alis Oancea, Gemma E. Derrick, Nuzha Nuseibeh, and Xin Xu
Edward Elgar Publishing, 2024

This book brings together perspectives on how research is organised, governed, evaluated, and experienced across different institutional, disciplinary, national, and global settings. It offers a broad and critical examination of meta-research, or research on research.

Citation: Oancea, A., Derrick, G. E., Nuseibeh, N., & Xu, X. (Eds.). (2024). Handbook of Meta-Research. Edward Elgar Publishing.

Changing Higher Education in East Asia

Edited by Simon Marginson and Xin Xu
Bloomsbury Academic, 2022

This book examines how higher education across East Asia is changing through internationalisation, indigenisation, mobility, governance, and regional cooperation. It brings together perspectives from across the region to understand these transformations in their distinctive historical, cultural, and global contexts.

The book received an Honourable Mention in the 2024 CIES Higher Education SIG Best Book Award.

Citation: Marginson, S., & Xu, X. (Eds.). (2022). Changing Higher Education in East Asia. Bloomsbury Academic.
